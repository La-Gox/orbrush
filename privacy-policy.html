Orb Rush — Politika privatnosti

Važi od: 9. oktobra 2026. · Programer: LaGox · Aplikacija: Orb Rush (com.lagox.orbrush)

Orb Rush („Igra“) je besplatna igra za jednog igrača za Android telefone. Ova politika objašnjava kojim podacima Igra rukuje.

Ukratko: Igra ne prikuplja, ne šalje i ne deli nikakve lične podatke.

Podaci koje prikupljamo

Nikakve. Igra nema naloge ni prijavu, nema reklama, nema kupovina u aplikaciji, nema analitiku i ne šalje izveštaje o greškama. Igra ne traži dozvolu za pristup internetu, pa ne može da šalje podatke sa uređaja.

Podaci koji se čuvaju na tvom uređaju

Da bi Igra zapamtila dokle si stigao/la, na telefonu se čuva fajl sa napretkom: pređeni nivoi, zvezdice i najbolji rezultati, novčići i pojačanja, stanje dnevne nagrade i dnevnog izazova, dostignuća, statistika igranja, viđeni saveti i podešavanja (zvuk, muzika, vibracija, jezik). Fajl se nalazi u privatnom folderu Igre na uređaju i Igra ga nikada nikome ne šalje.

  • Napredak brišeš u Igri: Podešavanja → Resetuj napredak (podešavanja ostaju).
  • Deinstalacijom Igre brišu se svi njeni podaci sa uređaja.
  • Ako na telefonu imaš uključenu Android rezervnu kopiju (backup), sistem Android može da uključi ovaj fajl u rezervnu kopiju na tvom Google nalogu, prema tvojim podešavanjima. Tim kopijama upravlja Google; programer im nema pristup.

Jezik

Pri prvom pokretanju Igra lokalno proverava jezik telefona da bi izabrala srpski ili engleski. Ta informacija se ne šalje nigde. Jezik možeš da promeniš u podešavanjima Igre.

Dozvole uređaja

  • Vibracija – kratki haptički odzivi tokom igre. Isključuje se u podešavanjima Igre.

Igra ne traži pristup internetu, lokaciji, kameri, mikrofonu, kontaktima, fajlovima ni obaveštenjima.

Treće strane

Igra je napravljena u Unity endžinu. Ova verzija ne sadrži Unity Analytics, servise za reklame, prijavu grešaka ni bilo koji drugi servis trećih strana koji prikuplja podatke. Igru preuzimaš i ažuriraš preko Google Play-a, na koji se primenjuje Google-ova politika privatnosti; Google programeru može da prikaže samo zbirnu, anonimnu statistiku (npr. broj instalacija i učestalost padova aplikacije).

Deca

Igra je pogodna za sve uzraste i ni od koga, pa ni od dece, ne prikuplja podatke.

Izmene

Ako neka buduća verzija doda funkciju koja rukuje podacima (na primer online funkcije ili reklame), ova politika biće ažurirana pre objave te verzije, sa novim datumom na vrhu.

Kontakt

Pitanja o privatnosti: goran@lagox.rs

Orb Rush — Privacy Policy

Effective: October 9, 2026 · Developer: LaGox · App: Orb Rush (com.lagox.orbrush)

Orb Rush (“the Game”) is a free single-player game for Android phones. This policy explains what data the Game handles.

In short: the Game does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

Data we collect

None. The Game has no accounts or sign-in, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no crash reporting. The Game does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot send data from your device.

Data stored on your device

To remember your progress, the Game keeps a save file on your phone: completed levels, stars and best scores, coins and boosters, daily reward and daily challenge status, achievements, play statistics, tips already shown and your settings (sound, music, vibration, language). The file is kept in the Game's private folder on your device and the Game never sends it to anyone.

  • You can delete your progress in the Game: Settings → Reset progress (your settings are kept).
  • Uninstalling the Game removes all of its data from your device.
  • If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include this file in your device backup in your Google account, according to your settings. Those backups are managed by Google; the developer has no access to them.

Language

On first start the Game checks your phone's language locally to choose Serbian or English. This information is not sent anywhere. You can change the language in the Game's settings.

Device permissions

  • Vibration – short haptic feedback during play. It can be turned off in the Game's settings.

The Game does not request access to the internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts, files or notifications.

Third parties

The Game is built with the Unity engine. This version contains no Unity Analytics, no advertising services, no crash reporting and no other third-party service that collects data. You download and update the Game through Google Play, which is covered by Google's Privacy Policy; Google may show the developer only aggregated, anonymous statistics (for example the number of installs and crash rates).

Children

The Game is suitable for all ages and does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds a feature that handles data (for example online features or ads), this policy will be updated before that version is released, with a new date at the top.
